Government Schemes for Farmers
Central & state government benefits, subsidies and programs
Central Govt.
State (Maharashtra)
Insurance
Financial Support
PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
Direct income support of ₹6,000/year in 3 installments to all farmer families. No middlemen — directly to bank account.
₹6,000/year→ pmkisan.gov.in
Soil Health
Soil Health Card Scheme
Free soil testing and health cards providing nutrient status of soil across 12 parameters with crop-wise fertilizer recommendations.
Free→ soilhealth.dac.gov.in
Credit Support
PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
Comprehensive crop insurance covering pre-sowing to post-harvest losses due to natural calamities, pests and diseases.
Low Premium→ pmfby.gov.in
Irrigation
PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana
Har Khet Ko Pani — ensuring irrigation access to every farm. 55 lakh ha brought under micro-irrigation. 90% subsidy for small/marginal farmers.
90% subsidy→ pmksy.gov.in
Technology
Digital Agriculture Mission 2025
Agristack — digital identity for farmers. AI/ML crop advisory, drone-based monitoring, satellite-based soil analysis at village level.
New 2024→ agricoop.nic.in
Market Access
e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)
Online trading platform connecting 1,361 mandis across 23 states. Farmers can sell from home, get better price discovery.
Live→ enam.gov.in
Maharashtra
Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Shetkari Karzmukti
Farm loan waiver scheme for Maharashtra farmers. Up to ₹2 lakh agricultural loan waived for eligible farmers.
₹2L waiver→ maha.gov.in
Maharashtra
Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi
Additional ₹6,000/year to farmers on top of PM-KISAN. Total ₹12,000/year direct benefit to Maharashtra farmers.
₹12,000/yr total→ nsmn.mahait.org
Maharashtra
Govari Shetkari Yojana
Subsidised seeds, fertilisers and irrigation equipment for tribal and small farmers in Vidarbha and Marathwada regions.
50% subsidy
Maharashtra
Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
Formation support, equity grants up to ₹15 lakh and matching equity for FPO formation in Maharashtra. Collective bargaining power.
₹15L grant→ fpc.org.in
Insurance
PMFBY — Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana
Kharif: 2% premium. Rabi: 1.5% premium. Annual commercial crops: 5%. Full coverage for yield loss, post-harvest, localised calamity.
Min 2% premium→ pmfby.gov.in
Insurance
Restructured Weather Based Crop Insurance
Coverage based on weather parameters — rainfall, temperature, humidity. Faster claims as based on weather station data, not yield.
Auto-claims→ pmfby.gov.in
Insurance
National Agricultural Insurance Scheme
Comprehensive risk coverage against crop failure due to natural calamities. All farmers including sharecroppers and tenant farmers covered.
All farmers
